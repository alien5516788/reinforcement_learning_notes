Stochastic Policy
    - Outputs a probability distribution over actions
      (In real implementations, outputs the action directly based on the probability)

      π(a | s)

Uniform Random policy
    - Type of stchastic policy
    - It assigns an equal probability to all the actions

      π(a | s) = 1 / |A|

ε-greedy Policy
    - Type of stochastic policy
    - It assigns highest probability to best action and divide remaining ε probability among all the actions

      π_ε(s, a) -> {
                    1 - ε + (ε / |A(s)|) ,  if a == arg max_a Q(s, a)
                    ε / |A(s)| ,            if a != arg max_a Q(s, a)
                 }


Deterministic Policy
    - Outputs an action
    - Each state has only one possible action with probability of 1

      μ(s)

Greedy Policy
    - Type of deterministic policy
    - Always chooses the best action

      π(s) -> arg max_a Q(s, a)


Optimal policy
    - Policy that gives the best possible expectd reward
    - Not a policy type
    - Goal of each policy is to become this

      π* -> Optimal Policy

There are other variations and optimizations as well,
Two general forms are,
    - Deterministic policy (μ)
    - Stochastic policy (π)
